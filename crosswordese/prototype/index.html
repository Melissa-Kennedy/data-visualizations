Sources: Rankings and counts are XWord Info's "Modern Era" tally of New York Times crossword answers, Nov 1993–2026 (the Will Shortz era, ~33 years). XWord Info's own preset windows are "Modern Era" and "All Time" (since 1942); neither is an exact trailing 50 years, so treat that framing as the puzzle's own working shorthand for its longest well-documented modern stretch, not a literal date range. Clues are real, historical NYT clues pulled from the xd crossword corpus (century-arcade/xd), filtered to Modern Era puzzles — up to six per word, sampled by how often each clue itself recurred. Several ranks are ties (e.g. #52 ECO/TEA, #82 ACRE/ETAL). Sketch, not final artwork.
